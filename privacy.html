Who looks after your data

Abigail Tracey, trading as A Brighter Humanity, is the data controller. Contact .

2

What we collect, and why

  • Daily note: your email address, with your consent, to send the note. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Orders: name, address, email and order details, to fulfil our contract with you and meet tax and accounting duties.
  • Membership: name, email, plan and billing history, to provide your membership.
  • Messages: whatever you send us, to reply.
3

Payments

Card details go directly to our payment provider. We never receive your full card number.

4

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run things: our payment provider (Stripe), shop platform (Shopify), email provider, delivery companies and website host. They act on our instructions.

5

YouTube

Videos load only when you press play. At that point YouTube may set cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy.

6

Cookies and storage

We store your shopping bag and sign-in on your own device so the site works. We don’t use advertising cookies. If we add analytics that need consent, we’ll ask first.

7

How long we keep it

Email addresses until you unsubscribe. Order and billing records for six years, for tax purposes. Account details until you close your account.

8

International transfers

Some providers are based outside the UK. Where they are, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires.

9

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or move your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

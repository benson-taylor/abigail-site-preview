A Brighter Humanity with Abigail Tracey

About Abigail

She trained as a lawyer. Then she jumped off the conveyor belt.

Abigail Tracey did the sensible thing in the UK: she trained in law, and the path ahead was all laid out. Then she stepped off it, towards the question that had been nagging her all along.

Now she asks one question, of the brightest women she can find: what makes a human life actually flourish?

A Brighter Humanity is where she goes looking for the answer, across academics, music, art, fashion, love and life, in a great many conversations with brilliant women.
